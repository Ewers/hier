Who We Are

Standalone Media Buying Division

Bidfactory is a standalone media buying division that has worked in performance marketing and mobile app user acquisition for ten years. The buying arm has run for three years and now operates under its own name.

We buy on our own accounts across Meta, Google, TikTok, Snapchat, Pinterest, Taboola/Realize, Unity, Moloco and AppLovin, at over $1M in monthly media spend.

Thirteen people run the buying floor — ten buyers, an analyst, a technical specialist and a buying director — with two business development managers and an in-house finance and operations team behind them. We work with advertisers and affiliate platforms on CPA, revenue share and fixed commission fee.

Ways to Cooperate

Model Who it fits What we take on How the payout is calculated
CPA Advertisers with a clearly defined target action: FTD, approved lead, sale, install. Media budget, accounts, creatives, landing pages, optimization. Budget risk sits with us. Fixed payout per approved action. Geo, caps and approval rules are agreed before launch and do not change mid-flight.
RevShare / Profitshare Advertisers with strong back-end monetization — iGaming, fintech, subscription products. Full media buying cycle plus the media budget. We carry the loss if the cohort does not pay back. An agreed share of net revenue, or of profit after media cost. Reconciled monthly, settled net-30.
Fixed commission fee Advertisers who keep the media budget on their side and buy management, not traffic. Campaign management, creative production, optimization on the advertiser's budget and structure. A percentage of managed spend, payable when the agreed ROI/ROAS payback KPI is met.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you resell traffic from other affiliates?

No. We work on our own agency accounts and our own Meta social profiles and setups, and the media budget is spent by our buyers. That is why we can name the source of every conversion.

What happens if the campaign does not hit the payback target?

On CPA and revenue share the media budget is ours, so the loss is ours. On a fixed commission fee the fee is payable only when the agreed ROI/ROAS KPI is met.

How much of the traffic breakdown do we actually see?

Spend, source, geo and post-click events are available in real time on both sides. What we do not hand over is account structure, setups and creative production assets.

As a buyer, how exactly is my profit share calculated?

The base is your campaigns' profit after media cost. The formula is fixed in the offer before you start and does not change retroactively.

Is a 24-hour launch realistic?

It is, once the offer, geo, cap and postbacks are agreed — accounts, creatives and landing pages are already in place on our side. The delay is almost always on tracking setup, not on media.

Tell us what you need bought, or what you can buy.

Advertisers get terms and a launch plan; buyers get a conversation about conditions and stack.
